Buyer enquiry notice & information handling

Your enquiry, not a commitment. Provide accurate contact and organization details and share only information you are authorized to disclose. Do not include confidential acquisition plans, privileged advice, personal identity documents or restricted technical information. This form is not an NDA.

How the information is used. IIPLA uses your name, organization, contact details, stated requirements and permission records to assess and respond to this enquiry, verify the organization, arrange follow-up and administer the service. Access is limited to personnel and contracted providers supporting those purposes, subject to appropriate obligations. Records are kept for active handling and follow-up, then only as needed for legal, contractual or recordkeeping obligations, before deletion or de-identification.

No automatic sharing or access. Submission does not grant access to owners' identities or confidential portfolios, create a public request, or authorize automatic forwarding of your identity or requirements to patent owners or other counterparties. Any proposed sharing is discussed first. IIPLA may represent the patent owner in a prospective transaction; this enquiry does not appoint IIPLA as your representative, create an attorney-client relationship, or incur a fee or exclusivity commitment. A suitable portfolio, offer or outcome is not guaranteed.

Your contact choices. Follow-up permission is limited to phone or email contact about this enquiry. There is no newsletter subscription, unrelated marketing consent, or permission for automated marketing calls or texts. These enquiry-specific limits apply even where the general Privacy Policy describes broader services. To change your contact preferences, stop follow-up or make a privacy request, contact IIPLA and mention your Buyer & Partner Enquiry. Applicable legal obligations and privacy rights continue to apply.
